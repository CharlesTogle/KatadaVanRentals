# Katada Van Rentals — Weekend Update (Aug 2–5, 2026)

## Summary

34 commits from Saturday to Monday. Focused on admin power features, booking flow polish, and infrastructure hardening.

---

## Customer-Facing Features

### Booking Flow Redesign
- New **all-in rental type** with route-based pricing (fuel + tolls included in quote)
- **Date & time picker** with split date/time selection for pickup and return
- **Location selector** with autocomplete powered by OpenRouteService
- **Toll plaza confirmation** component so customers can verify toll stops
- **With-driver support** — booking form now handles self-drive and with-driver modes
- **Booking mode column** in database for self-drive vs. with-driver distinction
- Self-drive pickup/dropoff addresses now stored as **structured JSONB** (not free text)
- **Fuel & toll estimates excluded** from the displayed booking total (transparent pricing)
- **Customer cancellation** now allowed for bookings in `pending_price_approval` status
- Cancellation and rejection **reasons** captured and stored
- **Manual pricing for out-of-area bookings** — pickups outside service area skip auto-pricing; admin sets price, customer accepts & pays downpayment
- **Payment details panel** — shows provider, account number, instructions, and QR code when a payment method is selected

### Booking Detail & Management
- Full **redesign of booking detail pages** (both customer and admin) with richer sections
- **Invoice PDF download** — customers can now download a proper invoice
- **Customer feedback** displayed on booking detail
- **Requested document types** — system now tracks which documents are needed per booking
- **Document viewer** with inline receipt viewing (no more downloading to check)

### Profile & Auth
- **Forgot password** and **reset password** pages
- **Email verification** simplified to a post-redirect confirmation
- **Password strength validation** with live requirement indicators
- **Client-side auth rate limiting** to prevent brute force
- **Expanded profile address fields** (street, suburb, city, state, postcode)
- Profile photo upload persistence

### Public Pages
- **Landing page** with FAQ section, contact section, and auth-aware navigation
- Contact, Privacy Policy, Terms pages with unified header
- **Dynamic branding** — business name and logo pulled from `app_settings` across all pages
